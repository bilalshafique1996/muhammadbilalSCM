  • Handle new product launches, samples, and packaging with suppliers

Demand Planning and Forecasting

Forecasts by SKU and channel that the buying plan is built on.

  • Forecast from sales history, trends, and seasonality by SKU and channel
  • Build scenarios when demand shifts or a supplier hits a capacity limit
  • Compare forecasts with actual sales and re-plan before the next buy

Inventory Management

Enough stock to keep selling, without paying to store stock that sits.

  • Set order timing and quantities from stock cover, lead times, and open POs
  • Find high-volume, slow-selling SKUs that drive FBA storage and aged inventory fees
  • Manage FBA capacity requests and restock low-stock listings across several seller accounts
  • Coordinate stock across Amazon FBA and 3PL warehouses in the US and EU

Logistics and Imports

Getting stock from the factory to the warehouse on time and at the right cost.

  • Coordinate freight forwarders, customs brokers, and 3PLs from origin to warehouse
  • Choose DDP or DDU for each shipment after comparing rates, duties, and the impact on COGS
  • Optimize master cartons to cut the volume (CBM) each shipment takes up

Product and Cost Data

Clean SKU and cost data, so forecasts, POs, and margins can be trusted.

  • Maintain the supplier master data sheet and SKU costs
  • Track landed costs, including freight and duties
  • Test supply chain data and tasks for the Odoo ERP go-live

Supply Chain Tools and Automation

Tools that replace manual work and find savings in the data.
